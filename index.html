Data
Foundations

Building the data foundation GTM decisions actually need

Every downstream GTM decision — account lists, territory design, segmentation, quota planning, even hiring — inherits whatever quality the underlying data has. Most organisations underestimate how much of that foundation has quietly drifted, and by the time it surfaces as a mis-sized quota or a bad territory, the root cause is hard to trace. Building the data governance function and a global data stewardship team to fix this at the source, not patch it downstream.

Pipeline
Generation

Finding revenue that isn't visible in the pipeline yet

Cold accounts — no active opportunity, no recent touch, nothing to signal potential — are treated as dead weight in most coverage models. But "cold" and "no potential" aren't the same thing; most models just can't tell the difference. Building an ML-based propensity model, layered with ecosystem signals, to identify real cross-sell and upsell potential inside that cold-account population and convert it into generated pipeline.

Country-Lens
Reviews

Business reviews that actually inform growth strategy

Most business reviews aggregate performance at a global or regional level — exactly the altitude at which the signals that matter to M&A and growth decisions disappear. A country performing well can mask a country performing badly, and vice versa. Building a business review structure with a country-level lens, granular enough that growth and M&A strategy can be informed by what's actually happening on the ground, not a blended average.
